Akwa Ibom

Akwa Ibom State is a beautiful state in southern Nigeria, located along the Gulf of Guinea. Its capital is Uyo. The state is known for its rich cultural heritage, peaceful environment, beautiful landscapes, and natural resources, especially crude oil and natural gas. Its people are known for their hospitality, delicious cuisine, traditional festivals, and vibrant culture.
